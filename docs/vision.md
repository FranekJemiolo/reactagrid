# ReactaGrid: Vision & Design Philosophy

## The Philosophy of Empirical Sandboxes

Traditional "falling sand" games offer entertaining cellular automata heuristics—fire burns wood, water extinguishes fire, acid dissolves metal. However, these rules are typically game-specific approximations without physical basis or quantitative consistency.

**ReactaGrid bridges granular physics and real-world chemistry.** It models particles not as arbitrary game sprites, but as discrete molecular packets governed by:

- **Empirical Densities:** Denser liquids sink beneath lighter liquids; particulate solids stack based on natural friction and gravity.
- **Thermodynamic Laws:** Heat conducts across cells based on specific heat capacities and thermal conductivity coefficients; liquids boil into expanding gases when reaching empirical boiling points.
- **Stoichiometric Reactions:** Real-world enthalpy changes ($\Delta H$), balanced mole fractions, activation energies, and hazardous gas emissions derived from verified chemical databases.

## Educational Value & Safety Protocols

ReactaGrid turns consumer chemistry into an interactive sandbox where players learn the actual composition of everyday supermarket products:

- Why mixing Bleach ($\text{NaClO}$) and Window Cleaner ($\text{NH}_3$) generates toxic chloramine gas ($\text{NH}_2\text{Cl}$).
- Why Baking Soda ($\text{NaHCO}_3$) and Vinegar ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$) produce an energetic release of pressurized $\text{CO}_2$ gas.
- How iron filings slowly oxidise into rust ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$) in the presence of water and ambient oxygen.

By rewarding players with Research Grants and unlocking detailed Discovery Journal entries, ReactaGrid transforms safety warnings and chemical properties into engaging gameplay discoveries.
